Live captions for what you hear and what you say

sososo is a free, open-source desktop app for your calls. It captions the call audio and your microphone as separate speakers, can save the meeting window as a video, and turns each session into a summary you can ask questions about.

No account and no sososo server. Your API keys stay in the OS keychain.

Keep the meeting window as a video

Turn on Record video, pick the window from a thumbnail grid, and sososo saves an MP4 next to the transcript, with your mic and the call audio mixed in. It plays back on the session page.

Windows and macOS. If video capture hits a problem, the transcript keeps going.

Press finish, then ask what you missed

Every session ends with an AI summary: an overview, the key points and the action items. Next to the transcript sits a chat that answers from what was actually said, and it keeps its history with the session.

Runs on the model you pick

  • OpenAI/
  • Anthropic Claude/
  • Google Gemini/
  • DeepSeek/
  • Grok/
  • Kimi/
  • GLM/
  • OpenRouter and other OpenAI-compatible endpoints/
  • Llama on your own machine

Choose one in Settings and paste your own key. A local Llama through Ollama, LM Studio or llama.cpp needs no key at all, and your summaries and chat never leave the computer.

No sososo server sits in the middle

The app talks to the services you set up, with your own keys, and to nothing else. Here is everything that leaves your computer, and where the rest stays.

  1. Audio

    → Deepgram

    Streamed over a secure WebSocket for live speech-to-text.

  2. Transcript text

    → Your AI provider

    Only when you ask for a summary, a translation or a chat answer. With a local Llama it stays on your computer.

  3. API keys

    → Your OS keychain

    Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain or the Linux Secret Service. Never a config file. They leave only as auth headers to the services above.

  4. Sessions

    → Your disk

    Transcripts, summaries and chats live in a local SQLite file.

  5. Accounts and telemetry

    None

    There is nothing to sign up for and nothing phones home.

After you install

  1. 1

    Open Settings and paste a free Deepgram API key. It's the one key you need for captions.

  2. 2

    If you want summaries, translation and chat, pick an AI provider and add its key, or point it at a local Llama.

  3. 3

    Press Ctrl+Alt+R, or the record button, and join your call.

A note on the first launch

The builds aren't code-signed yet, so your OS may warn you the first time. On Windows, choose More info, then Run anyway. On macOS, right-click the app and choose Open.
